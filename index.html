How I can help

Renting

Apartment hunting in New York can feel like a race. I'll make it an easy one, and I'll handle every step with you.

  • A search built around your budget, move date, commute and must-haves, in any borough
  • Scheduling tours, and an honest take on every place we see
  • Getting your application ready to go: pay stubs, ID, references and a guarantor if you need one
  • Reviewing the lease with you and staying with you right up to move-in day

Buying

Buying a home is one of the biggest steps you'll take. You deserve someone who's patient, honest and fully in your corner.

  • Getting you pre-approved through my small circle of trusted mortgage brokers
  • Finding homes that fit your life and budget, not just the listing photos
  • Writing strong offers and negotiating on your behalf
  • Guiding you through the board package, inspection and attorney review, all the way to closing

Selling

I price your home to get you the most for it and to get it sold without months of waiting.

  • A pricing plan based on recent sales nearby and what buyers are paying right now
  • Professional photos and marketing through Modern Spaces
  • Showings and open houses that put your home's best side forward
  • Negotiating every offer and keeping the sale on track to closing

Landlords

Your property is an investment, and the right tenant protects it. I find qualified tenants quickly and run everything exactly the way you expect.

  • Setting a rent that fills the unit fast without leaving money on the table
  • Marketing the apartment and handling every showing
  • Carefully screening applicants: income, credit and references
  • Coordinating the lease signing and move-in, with you kept in the loop at every step

New York moves fast. Good apartments go in a weekend, and the best co-ops never make the big sites. I'm out there every day, from Ditmars to downtown, so you hear about the place before it's gone.
